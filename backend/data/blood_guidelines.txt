BLOOD CONNECT - OFFICIAL BLOOD DONATION ELIGIBILITY & COMPATIBILITY GUIDELINES

1. BLOOD GROUP COMPATIBILITY CHART
- O- (Universal Donor): Can donate to O-, O+, A-, A+, B-, B+, AB-, AB+. Can only receive from O-.
- O+: Can donate to O+, A+, B+, AB+. Can receive from O-, O+.
- A-: Can donate to A-, A+, AB-, AB+. Can receive from O-, A-.
- A+: Can donate to A+, AB+. Can receive from O-, O+, A-, A+.
- B-: Can donate to B-, B+, AB-, AB+. Can receive from O-, B-.
- B+: Can donate to B+, AB+. Can receive from O-, O+, B-, B+.
- AB-: Can donate to AB-, AB+. Can receive from O-, A-, B-, AB-.
- AB+ (Universal Recipient): Can donate to AB+ only. Can receive from any blood group.

2. BASIC ELIGIBILITY CRITERIA
- Age: Must be between 18 and 65 years old.
- Weight: Must weigh at least 50 kg (110 lbs).
- General Health: Must feel well and be in good health on the day of donation. Do not donate if you have cold, flu, sore throat, or cold sores.
- Temperature & Blood Pressure: Normal temperature and pulse. Blood pressure should be within healthy limits (Systolic 90-180 mmHg, Diastolic 50-100 mmHg).
- Hemoglobin levels: Minimum 12.5 g/dl for females, 13.0 g/dl for males.

3. TEMPORARY DEFERRALS (WHEN YOU MUST WAIT TO DONATE)
- Tattoos and Body Piercings: Must wait 6 months from the date of getting a tattoo or body piercing.
- Travel History: Defer for 12 months after traveling to malaria-endemic regions.
- Recent Surgery or Illness: Must wait until fully recovered (usually 1 to 6 months depending on the procedure).
- Medications: Antibiotics require waiting at least 48-72 hours after finishing the full course.
- Pregnancy & Breastfeeding: Defer for 9 months after giving birth, or during breastfeeding.
- Alcohol/Drug Consumption: Do not consume alcohol for at least 24 hours prior to donation.
- Vaccine Deferrals: 
  * Live vaccines (e.g., MMR, Chickenpox): Wait 4 weeks.
  * Inactivated vaccines (e.g., Flu vaccine): No waiting period if feeling well.

4. PERMANENT DEFERRALS (WHEN YOU CAN NEVER DONATE)
- Tested positive for HIV (AIDS).
- Ever injected recreational drugs.
- Tested positive for Hepatitis B or Hepatitis C.
- Serious chronic heart disease, lung disease, or cancer.
- Hemophilia or other bleeding disorders.

5. PROCESS & PRE-DONATION ADVICE
- Drink plenty of water (at least 3-4 glasses) before donating.
- Eat a healthy, non-fatty meal 2-3 hours before donating.
- Avoid smoking for at least 2 hours before and after donation.
- Rest for 10-15 minutes after donation and drink juice/snacks.
- Avoid strenuous physical activity or heavy lifting for the rest of the day.
